This is a general privacy policy template. For App Store submissions, align this page with your in-app data practices (Apple “App Privacy” questionnaire) and your actual Firebase/AdMob configuration.

Summary

Scan-A-Bin helps you catalog household and small-business inventory. To provide the service, we may collect account identifiers (such as email/phone or Sign in with Apple), inventory content you add (bin names, rooms, item lists), and photos you upload for scanning. We use this data to operate the app, improve features, and support account and household sharing. If you enable ads or use AI scanning, third-party providers may process limited data to deliver those features.

We do

  • Store your inventory so you can search it later
  • Process photos you submit for AI item detection (when you request it)
  • Use security controls to protect accounts and household data

We don’t

  • Sell your personal information
  • Access your photos without your action (you choose what to upload)
  • Use your inventory content for advertising profiling outside the app

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account details (e.g., email address, phone number, or Sign in with Apple identifier), and basic profile details you choose to add (name, profile photo).
  • Inventory content (e.g., household name, rooms, bin labels/QR codes, item names, categories, quantities, notes).
  • Photos and images you upload for bin/item scanning or for item photos.
  • Invites and shared households (e.g., invitee identifiers and acceptance/decline status).

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data such as device type, OS version, app version, crash logs, and diagnostic performance data.
  • Usage data such as feature interactions (e.g., scans used, screens viewed), in aggregated form where possible.

Third-party SDK data

If enabled in your build, third-party services (for example, authentication, database/storage hosting, analytics, crash reporting, ads) may collect identifiers and diagnostic data to provide their services.

How We Use Information

  • Provide core functionality (store inventory, enable search, generate/resolve QR codes).
  • Enable household sharing (invites, roles/permissions, shared bin contents).
  • Provide AI-assisted features when you request a scan or analysis.
  • Improve reliability (debugging, crash diagnostics, performance optimization).
  • Communicate with you (support requests, important service updates).
  • Security and fraud prevention (protect accounts, prevent abuse).

Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only as needed to operate Scan-A-Bin and provide requested features:

  • With household members you invite (shared inventory content in the same household).
  • With service providers (cloud hosting, storage, analytics, crash reporting, email delivery, and similar vendors) who process data on our behalf.
  • For legal and safety reasons (to comply with law, enforce policies, or protect rights, property, and safety).
  • Business transfers (if a merger, acquisition, or asset sale occurs, data may transfer under appropriate safeguards).

We do not sell personal information.

AI & Photo Processing

When you choose to use AI scanning, photos you upload may be processed to detect items and extract inventory details. Processing may be performed using cloud AI services, and results are returned to your account to help populate item names and quantities.

Your control: AI processing only occurs when you submit an image for scanning within the app. You can delete bins/items and associated images from within the app (where supported).

We recommend you avoid uploading images containing sensitive personal information (e.g., government IDs, medical records). If you upload such content, it may be processed as part of the scan request.

Advertising

Scan-A-Bin may offer a free, ad-supported tier. If ads are enabled, advertising partners may collect device identifiers and limited usage data to serve and measure ads, and to support frequency capping and fraud prevention.

If you upgrade to an ad-free plan (where available), the app may reduce or disable ad-related data collection in accordance with your subscription status.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. If you delete content in the app (where supported), we will remove it from active systems within a reasonable period, subject to backup retention and legal requirements.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No system is 100% secure; you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and using up-to-date devices.

Children’s Privacy

Scan-A-Bin is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Access and update: You can update certain profile information within the app (where supported).
  • Delete content: You can delete bins/items and associated data within the app (where supported).
  • Account deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).
  • Marketing preferences: If you receive optional marketing emails, you can opt out via the link in those messages (if applicable).

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights (e.g., to object, restrict processing, or request portability). We will respond consistent with applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date and may provide additional notice within the app or on our website.
